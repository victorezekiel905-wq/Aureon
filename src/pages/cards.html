Card Questions

Good to know

How do I get an Aureon card?

Open an account, then go to Cards in Online Banking and choose New virtual card. Pick a name, the account it spends from, Visa or Mastercard, and a design. The card is ready immediately.

Can I see my full card number?

Yes. Select Show details on the card to reveal the full number, expiry date and CVV, and copy them for online checkout.

What happens when I freeze a card?

Every payment on that card is blocked until you unfreeze it. Your other cards and your accounts keep working as normal.

I lost my card. What should I do?

Freeze it straight away from the Cards page. If you do not find it, cancel the card and create a new one. If you see a payment you do not recognise, contact support from the app.
