Our Mission

Make good banking

Money should move as fast as you do, cost what it says, and be protected without getting in your way. Everything at Aureon starts from those three ideas.

That is why transfers between customers are free and instant, why every fee is listed on one page, and why large payments and loans are reviewed by people rather than left to chance.
