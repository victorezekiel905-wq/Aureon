How We Protect You

Six layers between
your money and harm

A PIN on every payment

A hashed 4-digit PIN authorises transfers and bills. Five wrong attempts lock it for 15 minutes.

Your data is yours alone

Row-level security in the database means no other customer's session can read your accounts or history.

Balances cannot be forged

Money moves only inside locked server-side transactions with balance and limit checks.

Large transfers reviewed

Payments above the review threshold are held until our team approves them.

Freeze in one tap

Freeze a card instantly, set limits, and switch online or contactless payments off.

Sign out everywhere

From Security settings, end every session on every device with one button.

Something Wrong?

Act fast in
four steps

1

Freeze your cards

Open Cards in Online Banking and switch on Freeze for every card.

2

Change your password

Go to Settings, then Security, and choose a new strong password.

3

Sign out everywhere

In Security settings, sign out of all devices so no one else stays logged in.

4

Tell us

Open a support request and describe what happened. We will review your account.
