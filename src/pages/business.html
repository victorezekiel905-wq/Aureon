How a supplier
payment works

Transfers between Aureon accounts are instant. Transfers to other banks, and anything above the review threshold, follow these steps.

You send it

Enter the supplier's bank, account number and amount, then confirm with your PIN.

It is held for review

The payment shows as pending while our team checks it. The money is reserved, not spent.

It is released

Once approved it completes, and you get a notification with the reference.
