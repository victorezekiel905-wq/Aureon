Accounts

How do I open an account?

Select Open Account, enter your name, email, country and a strong password, and choose your main currency. Your account number is issued immediately. Confirm your email, sign in, and set your transaction PIN.

How many accounts can I have?

Up to six accounts across USD, EUR, GBP, CAD and AUD. Each can be a current, savings or business account.

How do I verify my identity?

Go to Settings in Online Banking and submit your identity document. Our team reviews it, usually in less than a day, and you get a notification with the result.

Where do I find my account number?

On the Accounts page, or select Receive on the overview. You can copy it with one tap.

Transfers

How fast are transfers?

Transfers between Aureon accounts arrive instantly. Transfers above the review threshold, and transfers to other banks, are processed after a quick review by our team. You are notified the moment they complete.

Why is my transfer pending?

It is being reviewed, either because it is above the review threshold or because it goes to another bank. The money is reserved while it waits. Check its status in Activity.

What does a transfer cost?

Transfers between Aureon accounts are free. Transfers to other banks have a small percentage fee with a minimum. See the Rates page for today's figures.

Cards

I lost my card. What should I do?

Freeze it straight away from Cards in Online Banking. If you do not find it, cancel it and create a new card. If you see payments you do not recognise, contact support.

Can I set a spending limit?

Yes. Each card can have its own spending limit, and you can switch online and contactless payments on or off.

Security

What is the transaction PIN?

A 4-digit code you choose that authorises payments to other people. It is stored as a one-way hash, and five wrong attempts lock it for 15 minutes. Moving money between your own accounts does not need it.

I forgot my PIN.

Go to Settings, then Security, in Online Banking to change it. You will need to be signed in.

Someone asked me for my PIN.

Do not share it. Aureon will never ask for your PIN or password. Follow the steps in the Security Centre.

Savings & Loans

How is savings interest paid?

Savings accounts earn the APR shown on the Rates page. Interest is paid monthly and appears in your activity.

Can I repay a loan early?

Yes, at any time and with no early-repayment fee. Use Make a repayment on the Loans page.
