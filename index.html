FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast are transfers?

Transfers between Aureon accounts arrive instantly. Transfers above the security review threshold, and transfers to other banks, are processed after a quick review by our team — you'll get a notification the moment they complete.

What is the transaction PIN?

A 4-digit code you choose that authorises payments to other people. It is stored as a one-way hash, and five wrong attempts lock it for 15 minutes. Moving money between your own accounts doesn't need it.

Can I hold more than one currency?

Yes. Open up to six accounts in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD or NGN, and move money between them at the live rate shown in the app.

What happens if I lose my card?

Freeze it instantly from the Cards page, then cancel it and create a new virtual card in seconds.

How do I get help?

Open Support in the app to message our team. You'll get a notification as soon as we reply.
